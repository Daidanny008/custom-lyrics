Arise!
You who refuse to be slaves!
Let our flesh and our blood
Be forged into a new Great Wall!
The Chinese nation has come to its hour of greatest peril;
From every throat the last cry breaks forth.
Arise!
Arise!
Arise!
We, a multitude of one heart,
Braving the enemy's fire, march on!
Braving the enemy's fire, march on!
March on!
March on!
On!
