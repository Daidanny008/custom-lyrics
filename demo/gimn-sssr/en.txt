An unbreakable union of free republics
Great Rus' has joined together for ever.
Long live the creation of the peoples' will,
The united, the mighty Soviet Union!

Be glorious, our free Fatherland,
Sure stronghold of the friendship of peoples!
The Soviet banner, the people's banner,
Let it lead from victory to victory!

Through storms the sun of freedom shone upon us,
And the great Lenin lit our way:
Stalin raised us to be true to the people,
And inspired us to labour and to great deeds!

Be glorious, our free Fatherland,
Sure stronghold of the happiness of peoples!
The Soviet banner, the people's banner,
Let it lead from victory to victory!

We raised our army up in the fighting.
We shall sweep the vile invaders from our road!
In battle we decide the fate of generations,
We shall lead our Fatherland to glory!

Be glorious, our free Fatherland,
Sure stronghold of the glory of peoples!
The Soviet banner, the people's banner,
Let it lead from victory to victory!
