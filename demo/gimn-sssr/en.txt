An unbreakable union of free republics
Great Rus' has joined together for ever.
Long live the creation of the peoples' will,
The united, the mighty Soviet Union!

Be glorious, our free Fatherland,
Sure stronghold of the friendship of peoples!
The party of Lenin, the strength of the people,
Leads us to the triumph of communism!

Through storms the sun of freedom shone upon us,
And the great Lenin lit our way:
He raised the peoples to the righteous cause,
And inspired us to labour and to great deeds!

Be glorious, our free Fatherland,
Sure stronghold of the friendship of peoples!
The party of Lenin, the strength of the people,
Leads us to the triumph of communism!

In the victory of the immortal ideas of communism
We see the future of our country,
And to the Red Banner of our glorious Fatherland
We shall always be selflessly true!

Be glorious, our free Fatherland,
Sure stronghold of the friendship of peoples!
The party of Lenin, the strength of the people,
Leads us to the triumph of communism!
